Privacy Policy for Swasthya Health

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Swasthya Health collects personal information such as your email address (via authentication), health and fitness metrics (via Google Health Connect), and medical document files that you explicitly upload to manage your health data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used to provide app functionality, analyze health records securely via our Cloud Run backend, and maintain your user profile.

3. Data Security

All user data is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS protocols and stored securely within our authenticated Firebase database environment.

4. Data Deletion & Account Removal

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data stored on our servers. To request account and data deletion, please send an email to our support team:

Email: kushagramakin@gmail.com

Please include your account email address and "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line. Once received, our team will permanently delete your user account, authentication records, and stored files within 7 business days.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to us at kushagramakin@gmail.com.
