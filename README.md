Privacy Policy for Swasthya Health
Effective Date: August 31, 2026

1. Data Collection and Use
Swasthya Health collects the following user data to provide its core functionality:

Personal Information: Name and Email Address are collected for account authentication and user profile management via Google Sign-In.

Health and Fitness Data: We request read access to steps and sleep data (via Health Connect) exclusively to populate the user's lifestyle dashboard and calculate baseline health trajectories.

Files and Documents: Medical and lab reports uploaded by the user are securely processed to extract biomarker data for the personal health dashboard.

2. Data Security and Sharing
All transmitted data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell user data. Extracted health data is stored securely in Firebase under the user's unique authentication ID.

3. Medical Disclaimer
Swasthya Health is a tracking and logging application. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
